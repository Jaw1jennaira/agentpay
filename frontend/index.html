Demo walkthrough (end-to-end, testnet)

  1. Connect MetaMask on BSC Testnet (chain 97) with tBNB from the faucet.
  2. Merchant (Agent ID holder): Create Invoice tab — fill in the .ai name, amount, token, memo → create the invoice, note its ID.
  3. Customer: Pay tab — check the merchant's .ai name, load the invoice by ID, Approve (for MockUSDT), then Pay. Check the tx link on BscScan testnet.
  4. Tip: Tip tab — send tBNB to any .ai name with a memo.
  5. Dashboard: the merchant sees their invoice turn Paid, with the payer.

Non-custodial: funds never pass through our server — all payments go wallet-to-wallet through the AgentPay contract. Never share your private key / seed phrase with anyone.
